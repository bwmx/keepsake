Set up a LocalNet environment

This deploys the EpochCommittee app, which implements the paper's evolving committees (eprint 2020/464 §3), and enrolls registry members, then runs a worker for each. Every epoch, members privately win VRF sortition on the block seed and nominate the next committee's anonymous seats. Then each holder of the current committee posts one handover message from its own account: re-sharings of every bucket key, contributions to new buckets, and decryption partials for capsules. Then it erases everything. Capsules are locked under the key of the bucket their expiry falls in. All accounts are LocalNet throwaways.

Any t seats of a committee can use a bucket key; fewer cannot. A committee also needs t honest seats online to hand over, or its buckets are lost. Epochs are 80 rounds: nominations, then handover, then verification.

Sender

Encrypts locally under the key of the bucket its expiry falls in, taken from the state the committee attested, and registers u, a proof bound to this policy, and the payload hash. No dealer, no per-capsule committee.

      Activity

        Recipient

        The recipient binds one fresh X25519 key on-chain. At the next handover each holder seals its partial to that key. The recipient checks the partials against the attested commitments, needs t of them, and decrypts locally.

        Members

        Click a member to take it offline or bring it back (an outage: it keeps its memory, its seats stay silent, and it catches up when back). A committee hands over as long as t of its seats post.
